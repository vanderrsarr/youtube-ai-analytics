Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. About This Application

YouTube AI Analytics is a private application designed to analyze YouTube channel performance and support automated content optimization.

2. Information Accessed

With authorization from the YouTube channel owner, the application may access:

  • Channel and video information
  • Video views and engagement metrics
  • Watch time and audience retention
  • Available audience demographics
  • Subscriber growth and performance data

3. How Information Is Used

The data is used for channel performance monitoring, analytics reporting, content research, and improving future video strategies.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authorized data may be processed and stored on privately managed computing infrastructure. Access is restricted to authorized operators. OAuth credentials are not publicly disclosed.

5. Data Sharing

YouTube data may be processed by authorized AI services to generate analytics reports and content recommendations. Data is not sold to advertisers.

6. Google and YouTube Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using the application, users are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

Users can review Google's Privacy Policy .

7. Revoking Access

Users may revoke the application's access through their Google Account connections settings .
